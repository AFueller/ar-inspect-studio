# Roadmap

## Near term
- Complete and document physical Meta Quest 3/3S acceptance for v26.1.
- Improve calibration robustness and visual feedback.
- Extend authoring toward polygon/free-form planar regions.
- Improve text size, hit targets and movable/recenterable MR panels.
- Expand automated security and compatibility checks.

## Medium term
- Broader browser/runtime compatibility testing.
- More regression coverage for calibration, persistence and revision conflicts.
- Synthetic example scenarios for education and industrial QA.
- Optional computer-vision-assisted alignment that remains transparent and non-blocking.

## Longer term
- Richer spatial annotations and non-rectangular inspection regions.
- Interoperable scenario import/export.
- Optional integrations with maintenance, asset and training systems.
